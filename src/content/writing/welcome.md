This is a **starter post**. It is `draft: true`, so it does not appear in the
`/writing` index, the nav, or the RSS feed — the whole Writing section stays
hidden until you publish a real post.

## How to add a post

1. Create `src/content/writing/my-post.md`.
2. Add front-matter: `title`, `description`, `pubDate` (YYYY-MM-DD), optional
   `tags`, and `draft: false` when you're ready to publish.
3. Write the body in Markdown. Headings, **bold**, _italic_, `code`, lists, and
   links all render.

## What supports your credibility here

Long-form writing is the single biggest signal a portfolio can carry for an
AI/ML engineer — a walkthrough of an LLM-agent eval harness, an MLOps
post-mortem, or a distributed-systems trade-off you lived through. Delete this
post and write that.
